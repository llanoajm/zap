Design A (GNN encoder → predicted cost parameters → differentiable zap OPF) outperforms the pure surrogate (P2) on cross-topology TEST states in two critical ways: it eliminates power balance violations entirely (0 vs 44%) and reduces cost gap by 2.4× (63% vs 154%), while maintaining comparable LMP prediction error (1.32 vs 1.33). JEPA pretraining with SIGReg anti-collapse regularization hurt rather than helped, because intra-graph node embedding correlations violate SIGReg's i.i.d. assumption and the EMA target encoder drifted faster than the predictor could track. The differentiable solver produces physically meaningful LMP dual signals (investment gradient direction cos_sim = 0.76 vs exact zap), but active-set mismatches from imperfect cost prediction cause 10× magnitude errors that make unreliable planning gradients. No warm-start speedup was achieved (0.97× of cold LP): the GNN changes LP parameters, not initial primal points. The architecture is sound; the gap is in upstream feature-to-cost-parameter mapping, not in solver integration.
